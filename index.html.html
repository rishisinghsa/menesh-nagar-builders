What We Build, Fix & Maintain

1. Construction

NHBRC-compliant residential builds, room additions, boundary walls, garages, foundations, and structural brickwork built to municipal plan specs.

2. Electrical

DB board upgrades, fault finding, plug and light installations, outdoor security lighting, surge protection, and rewiring for renovations.

3. Plumbing

Burst pipe repairs, geyser replacements and valves, blocked drains, bathroom and kitchen pipework, taps, cisterns, and water pressure fixes.

4. Paving

Driveways, walkways, pool surrounds, and courtyard paving. Proper ground compaction, stormwater runoff levels, edging, and sunken brick repairs.

5. Painting

Full interior and exterior house painting, roof coating, boundary wall painting, crack filling, damp priming, and high-pressure wall preparation.

6. Waterproofing

Torch-on waterproofing for flat concrete roofs and balconies, parapet wall sealing, ridge capping, valley flashing, and rising damp treatment.

7. Renovations

Complete kitchen and bathroom remodels, floor and wall tiling, ceilings, plastering, and full home modernisations managed from strip-out to handover.

8. Alterations

Opening up load-bearing walls with steel/concrete lintels, garage-to-flatlet conversions, patio enclosures, and reconfiguring existing layouts.

9. Maintenance & Repairs

General home and complex upkeep—broken doors and locks, sagging gutters, cracked plaster, insurance repair work, and pre-sale property touch-ups.
